# Reference claims — Danisi_2015

Source: `Danisi_2015_Elastic_Behaviour_and_pressure_induced_structural_modification.pdf`
("Elastic behavior and pressure-induced structural modifications of the
microporous Ca(VO)Si₄O₁₀·4H₂O dimorphs cavansite and pentagonite",
Danisi et al., Microporous and Mesoporous Materials, 2015).

**Written from the full text (abstract, introduction, methods, results,
discussion) before the run — these claims are cold.** This fixes the
claim-set-narrowness problem flagged for Evans, where abstract-only claims
systematically under-counted a detailed summary.

1. Cavansite and pentagonite are orthorhombic dimorphs of
   Ca(VO)(Si₄O₁₀)·4H₂O.
2. They were studied at high pressure by in situ synchrotron X-ray powder
   diffraction in a diamond anvil cell.
3. Two pressure-transmitting media were used: methanol:ethanol:water = 16:3:1
   (m.e.w.) and silicone oil (s.o.).
4. Cavansite data were collected to 8.17(5) GPa (m.e.w.) and 7.28(5) GPa (s.o.).
5. Compressional behavior is strongly anisotropic: compressing cavansite in
   s.o. to 7.28(5) GPa gives a 12.2% volume contraction, with a, b and c
   decreasing by 1.6%, 10.3% and 0.3%.
6. The main high-pressure deformation mechanism is variation of the T–O–T
   angles.
7. Pentagonite data were collected to 8.26(5) GPa (m.e.w.) and 8.35(5) GPa
   (s.o.); single-crystal experiments in m.e.w. reached 2.04(5) GPa.
8. Pressure-induced over-hydration (PIH) was observed in m.e.w.; a new H₂O
   molecule enters the structure and stiffens it.
9. Between 2.45(5) and 2.96(5) GPa in m.e.w., an orthorhombic-to-triclinic
   phase transition was observed.
10. In s.o., pentagonite transformed to a triclinic phase above 1.71(5) GPa.
11. Overall compressibility in s.o. is comparable between the two: 11.6%
    volume contraction for pentagonite vs 12.2% for cavansite.
12. The Birch–Murnaghan equation of state gives V₀ = 1300(1) Å³ and
    K₀ = 38.1(5) GPa.
13. Cavansite was refined in the standard space group Pnma, preferred over the
    Pcmn setting reported by Evans.
14. Pentagonite single-crystal data are consistent with space group Ccm2₁.
15. Samples came from Wagholi, Poona district, Maharashtra, India; powder
    experiments used the SNBL BM01A beamline at ESRF (λ = 0.682534 Å).
16. The study is described as the first high-pressure investigation of
    heteropolyhedral vanadosilicate frameworks.
17. The VO₅ square-based pyramids have a short apical V–O bond of ~1.6 Å and
    four basal bonds of ~2.0 Å.
18. In cavansite the tetrahedral sheets contain four- and eight-fold rings; in
    pentagonite there are only six-membered rings.
